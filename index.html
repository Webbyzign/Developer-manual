Islamic & Spiritual Wellness Habit Tracker

12-Week Non-Technical Execution Roadmap & Action Guide

​
Total Duration
12 Weeks (3 Months)
Target Output
iOS & Android MVP
Core Focus
Salah, Dhikr, Journal
Testing Window
Weeks 10 & 11
​
Strategic Focus: This roadmap maps out the exact non-technical step-by-step tasks required to plan, test, refine, and release your app within 90 days without writing code directly.
​
Phase 1

Research, Strategy & App Design (Weeks 1–3)

​

Week 1: Market Research & Feature Blueprint

Strategy & Vision
  • Competitor Analysis: Download 3–4 Islamic apps (Tarteel, Muslim Habits, Pillars, Habitica). Note down strengths, missing options, and screen layouts.
  • Define Unique Value: Clarify your core vision — combining prayer/dhikr tracking with daily mental wellness and personal reflection.
  • Categorize Features: Split planned features into MVP Essentials (Salah, Tasbeeh counter, journal, streaks) and Future Updates (community groups, audio recitations).
​

Week 2: User Journeys & Screen Sketches

App Flow & UX
  • Map User Steps: Outline the step-by-step path: Onboarding → Location Setup → Daily Home View → Complete Habit → Journal Entry.
  • Draft Screen Wireframes: Sketch key screens on paper or Figma: • Screen 1: Home Dashboard (Upcoming Prayer & Habit Checklist) • Screen 2: Tasbeeh / Dhikr Counter View • Screen 3: Daily Reflection & Gratitude Journal • Screen 4: Streak Calendar & Progress Stats
​

Week 3: Visual Identity & Prototype Finalization

UI Design & Themes
  • Brand Color & Typography: Select a calm spiritual palette (emerald green, warm cream, subtle gold, neutral dark slate).
  • Design High-Fidelity Views: Finalize exact screen designs in Figma so you have a complete visual model before build phase.
  • Task Management Setup: Organize a project board in Notion or Trello with weekly checklists.

Phase 2

Core App Building & Engine Setup (Weeks 4–8)

​

Week 4: User Accounts & Core Layout Structure

Foundation
  • Onboarding Flow: Build user registration, welcome intro screens, and login options (Email, Google, Apple ID).
  • App Navigation Bar: Setup the main tab bar at the bottom to easily switch between Home, Tasbeeh, Reflection, and Settings.
​

Week 5: Prayer Times & Daily Dashboard

Prayer API & Home
  • Location Services Integration: Enable auto-detection of user location to calculate exact local prayer times.
  • Build Main Dashboard: Display current Hijri date, next prayer countdown, and daily active habits in a clean list.
​

Week 6: Islamic & Wellness Habit Trackers

Habits Engine
  • Habit Checklists: Create tap-to-complete actions for daily Salah, Quran reading pages, and custom habits.
  • Habit Streaks System: Add daily counters to show consecutive completion days and build user consistency.
​

Week 7: Tasbeeh Counter & Reflection Journal

Spiritual Tools
  • Digital Tasbeeh Counter: Build a smooth counter screen with sound/vibration feedback, target counts, and quick reset.
  • Daily Journal Module: Create a daily writing space with mood check-ins (1–5) and prompts for spiritual gratitude.
​

Week 8: Reminders & Notification Setup

Notifications
  • Prayer Alerts: Configure automated notifications before or at each prayer time based on local times.
  • Daily Habit Nudges: Set customizable morning/evening reminders for Adhkar and daily reflection journaling.

Phase 3

Refinement, Testing & Bug Fixing (Weeks 9–10)

​

Week 9: Visual Polish & Offline Support

User Experience
  • Animations & Micro-Interactions: Add smooth checkmark completion animations and subtle haptic feedback.
  • Offline Functionality: Ensure prayer times, habit checks, and journal entries work fully without an active internet connection.
​

Week 10: Closed Beta Testing & Community Feedback

Beta Testing
  • Beta Build Distribution: Share test build with 15–30 testers (family, friends, community members) via TestFlight / Google Play.
  • Feedback & Bug Fixing: Collect user feedback on prayer time accuracy, notification reliability, and fix reported issues.
​
Phase 4

App Store Launch & Deployment (Weeks 11–12)

​

Week 11: Store Page Setup & Final QA

Preparation
  • App Store Listings: Write app descriptions, keywords, privacy policy terms, and design preview screenshots.
  • Final Quality Audit: Verify overall app performance, time zone updates, and notification triggers.
​

Week 12: Public Launch & Growth

Release
  • Production Launch: Submit and publish application on Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
  • Community Outreach: Share store links with personal networks, Islamic groups, and social channels.
​

Weekly Milestones Overview

Phase Weeks Key Milestone Output
1. Strategy & Design Weeks 1 – 3 Wireframes, Figma UI, Competitor Blueprint, Task Board
2. Core Building Weeks 4 – 8 Auth, Prayer Engine, Habit Streaks, Tasbeeh & Notifications
3. Testing & Polish Weeks 9 – 10 Offline Mode, 20-User Beta Release, Bug Resolution
4. Launch Weeks 11 – 12 Store Graphics, Apple/Google Approval, Public Release
